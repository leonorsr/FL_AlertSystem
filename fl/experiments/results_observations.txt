5. Cross-Technique Comparison

Best combination per evaluation technique

| Technique      | Best preprocessing    | Best model       | Candidate | PR-AUC   | Miss Rate | FAR      | Balanced Accuracy | F1       |
|----------------|-----------------------|------------------|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| baseline       | magnitude_features    | neural_network   | mlp_wide  | 0.995453 | 0.026596  | 0.021527 | 0.975939          | 0.966839 |
| byclient       | magnitude_features    | neural_network   | mlp_wide  | 0.993702 | 0.023047  | 0.027753 | 0.974600          | 0.958563 |
| onedatasetout  | magnitude_features    | xgboost          | xgb_deep  | 0.851410 | 0.275105  | 0.047459 | 0.838718          | 0.764376 |
| kfoldbyclient  | magnitude_features    | neural_network   | mlp_wide  | 0.991267 | 0.034965  | 0.025577 | 0.969729          | 0.958091 |

Most important global pattern
- magnitude_features is the strongest preprocessing strategy in every evaluation technique tested so far.
- neural_network (mlp_wide) is the best overall model in three out of four techniques.
- xgboost becomes the best candidate only in onedatasetout, the harshest domain-shift scenario.

Relative difficulty of the techniques
- baseline is the easiest setting and produces the strongest global metrics.
- byclient is slightly stricter, but performance remains very strong.
- kfoldbyclient is also strong and confirms the same overall winner with a more robust evaluation protocol.
- onedatasetout is by far the hardest setting and should be interpreted as the strongest test of cross-dataset robustness.

Interpretation
- The consistency between baseline, byclient, and kfoldbyclient is a strong sign that the selected preprocessing strategy is reliable.
- The fact that the winner changes in onedatasetout is not a contradiction; it indicates that domain shift across datasets stresses the models differently from client-level generalization.
- If the next stage is federated learning, the byclient and kfoldbyclient settings are the most relevant references, because they better reflect generalization across clients.
